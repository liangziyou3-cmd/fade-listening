PRIVACY

Your lesson stays close to you.

Fade Listening is a local first prototype. Lesson packages are read in your browser, and practice signals stay in the browser unless you choose to share them.

What we store

The demo does not require an account. Uploaded packages and session progress are used to run the lesson in your browser.

Questions

For project questions, contact the team that shared your lesson package.
